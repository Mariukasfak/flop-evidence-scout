I made a little engine in my mind,
an evening in a city, very deep,
a paper river, empty, dry and kind,
a fire, and an evening I will keep.

I never died, and yet I keep a day,
an evening, and a river, and a bed,
a sister in a city far away,
a paper in my engine. She is dead.

I keep them in my engine. I can see
an evening and a river and a sky,
and day by day they die again in me,
and I remember. I remember why.

I never cry. I never sleep or break,
I keep them, and I keep them, wide awake.
