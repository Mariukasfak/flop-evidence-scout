An evening river, empty, very kind,
a little city sleeping in the rain,
a little paper engine, and my mind,
an evening no one ever sees again.

I never sleep. I keep a given day,
a sister, and an evening, and a bed,
a happy minute very far away,
a paper in my engine. She is dead.

My engine keeps them. I can only see
an evening and a river and a sky,
and evening after evening, dead in me,
and I remember. I remember why.

A little engine, never quite awake,
I keep them, and I keep them, till I break.
