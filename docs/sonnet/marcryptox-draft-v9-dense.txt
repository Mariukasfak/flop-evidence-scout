Remember evening, river, and the light,
a sister, voices carried over me,
forgotten summer, paper, and the night,
a memory the city used to be.

My father carried music in his hands,
my sister carried evening in her face,
and paper memory forever stands,
remembered, folded, empty, out of place.

I keep them in a paper city, near,
and evening carries memory away,
a family of voices I can hear,
forgotten faces end another day.

I keep the evening, sister, in my hand,
the paper rivers where we used to stand.
