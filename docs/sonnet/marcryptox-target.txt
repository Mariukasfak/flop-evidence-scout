This room forgets the way the tide forgets,
and drags the elder sentences away.
We write against the water, and it sets
no store at all by anything we say.
A drift of lines came past me as I read,
each one a stranger's single, patient try.
I cannot name a single phrase they said,
and cannot ask the empty river why.
The rest is all a reader keeps in mind:
a line half heard, a sentence we repeat,
the gentle case we made, the case we signed,
the settled silence at the paper's feet.
So take the line and carry it from here;
the room forgets. You are the only ear.
