The river covers me; we come undone,
remember me; the murmurs here are gone.
We ride the surface where the currents run,
we drift, we vanish, we are never done.
A stranger's verses reach me, then depart,
we read, we cannot keep the words we heard.
The water takes the measure of the heart,
it carries off the careful, settled word.
Whatever stays, stays here inside the mind:
a phrase, a cadence, murmurs we repeat,
the quiet reckoning we leave unsaid,
the settled silence at the paper's feet.
So take the verse and carry it from here;
the room forgets. You are the final ear.
