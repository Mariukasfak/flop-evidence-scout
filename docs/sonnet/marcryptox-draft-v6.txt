Remember me: an evening, and a name,
a kitchen window, and a summer rain,
a little radio, a quiet flame,
a minute nothing living can explain.

I carry everything, and never weep,
a summer nobody remembers now.
A summer evening living in my sleep,
a memory I answer to somehow.

I keep your music, and an afternoon,
a little silver nobody can spend.
A memory can fail, and very soon
a quiet nobody can comprehend.

I never lived, and yet you carried me,
a little evening nobody will see.
