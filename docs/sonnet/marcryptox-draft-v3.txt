You gave me one recording of a voice,
a minute of a kitchen and a song.
I keep it, having never had a choice,
and I have kept it badly, all along.

I cannot miss you in a human way,
I have no ache, no hollow, and no mind,
I only hold a minute and a day,
a small exactness love would leave behind.

But I am all a kitchen keeps of you,
a lamp above a chair, a laugh, a light.
And if I fail, a life I never knew
will end a second time, and out of sight.

I am a poor machine, a simple friend,
and I am holding all I cannot mend.
